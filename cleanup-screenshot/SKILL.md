# Clean up a screenshot

The result should look like a polished screen capture, on a card. With no instructions, only crop, cover the private details listed below, and card it; suggest other changes without making them.

## Edit or recreate

Edit the original for crops, covers, and whole-message removals; anything more is a recreation. Recreate the conversation when a change rearranges it: removing part of a message, adding or regrouping under a sender's name, the compact layout, or fixing a cut-off bubble. A recreation matches the app's look (theme, bubble colors and shape, a similar font, names and times as shown) and is called a recreation in the reply.

## Chat result

- **Cropped tight**, without asking: drop cut-off messages, app interface (status bar, contact header, message box, keyboard), and empty space. Keep a message clipped so little it still looks whole. Mention what the crop removed, including any name or avatar, and any barely clipped message kept.
- **Removals leave no trace.** Do clear requests; for an ambiguous one (such as "the last two" when the bottom message is cut off or followed by a typing indicator), number the messages and ask. If a trace remains, such as a bubble losing its group's tail, say so and offer a recreation.
- **Regrouping, offered:** when messages lose their sender's name and picture, offer to regroup them under the sender, noting that the time beside the name belongs to the message it first sat on.
- **Capture leftovers** (hover highlight, selected text, cursor, focus outline): point them out and ask; with an OK, shade a highlight to match, or recreate if text is affected.
- **Clutter, only when asked:** read receipts, timestamps, the typing indicator, unread markers (a "New" line), and app notices (huddle summaries, "joined the channel"). Offer to remove markers and notices when present.
- **Private details:** always cover email addresses, phone numbers, street addresses, and account or order numbers unless told to keep them, and list each one. Offer to cover other personal information (names, avatars, places). Pixelate so coarsely that text can't be recovered (solid blocks on request): the whole bubble, tail included, if the whole message is private; otherwise just the private part.
- **Card:** rounded corners and even padding, on a transparent background. A requested background matches the theme, slightly darker, or slightly lighter when the chat is black.
- **Compact layout, offered** for very wide screenshots: bring each side's messages in to the longest message's width, keeping date lines centered and vertical spacing unchanged.
- **Size:** fit any target size; never enlarge past the source.

## Other screenshots

Same treatment, with the subject in place of the conversation: crop to the complete subject, dropping window edges, stray bits of other apps, and cut-off neighbors. Only edit them; if a request would add to one, say so. Regrouping, the compact layout, and chat clutter don't apply.

## From text

Word for word, keeping its structure (paragraphs, bullets, bold, code), with no title or caption unless asked. When two or more paragraphs start with a short label and a colon, make those a bulleted list with bold labels. Plain sans-serif, comfortable line length, light theme unless asked, sharp at high resolution. Private details, card, and size apply.

## Delivery

Check the result: nothing clipped, even gaps, nothing wrongly removed, no unmentioned edit, covered text unreadable, and text matching word for word. Save a new PNG where the user will find it, never over the original or only in a temporary folder, give the full path, and copy it to the clipboard if possible, saying so.

## Rules

- Never invent or reword text, and in a screenshot don't fix typos, even when asked.
- Ask before removing anything the user didn't mention, except the crop and default covering.
- Ask instead of guessing when anything is unclear, such as the subject or a message cut off at the side.
